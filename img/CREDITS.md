Each candidate photo was opened and visually inspected (not just license-checked)
before use, and rejected if it showed a real identifiable person or a real
company's own branding/signage in frame. Several Wikimedia biomass-plant photos
were rejected on that basis (e.g. shots with "IOI Bio-Energy", "SVP / BioWarmteCentrale
de Purmer" or "Vitoria-Gasteiz / Giroa" signage visible, and a control-room /
harvest photo with real people in frame).

All operating figures on the site (128 MW, 96.1% availability, farm counts,
tariffs) are illustrative placeholders — not real data about any real facility.
