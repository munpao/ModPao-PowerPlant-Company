Ash return

Closing the loop, one batch at a time.

The plant produces roughly 18,000 tonnes of bottom and fly ash a year. All of it — no exceptions — goes back to registered fuel suppliers, free of charge, as a potassium and silica soil amendment. Each batch ships with a lab analysis so the supplier can decide where and how to apply it.

This is the closed-loop story the plant was built to tell: residue leaves the farm as fuel, comes back as amendment, instead of leaving the air as smoke.
