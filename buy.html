How to buy

Four steps from an enquiry to a signed PPA. Timelines below are the ones we hit — commercial team is scoped to hold them.

01

Load profile review

Send hourly or half-hourly consumption for twelve months. We model the firm block that fits it.

02

Indicative term sheet

Price, escalation basis, availability guarantee and liquidated damages, within ten working days.

03

Due diligence

Technical dossier, audited generation history and EIA returns released under NDA. Site visit arranged.

04

Execution

PPA signature, grid code compliance filing and metering commissioning. Typically 90–120 days to first delivery.
